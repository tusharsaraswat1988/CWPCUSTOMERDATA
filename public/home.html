Building Varanasi's Most Trusted Doorstep Auto & Solar Care

"Namaste Varanasi! For years, car owners in our holy city had only two frustrating choices: hand their keys to local colony cleaners who scratch the clear coat with dirty cloths and hard water, or spend half of Sunday waiting in long queues at crowded wash centers.

We founded CWP Detailers with a single uncompromising mission: to bring scientific, Zero-TDS demineralized RO water, scratch-free 2-bucket microfiber washing, and professional ceramic protection right to your home gate.

Today, over 1,200 Varanasi families wake up to a gleaming car every morning without lifting a finger. If you ever have any question or feedback, my team and I are just one WhatsApp tap away."
