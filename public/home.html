Founder's Note

Building Varanasi's Most Trusted Doorstep Auto & Solar Care

"Namaste Varanasi! For years, vehicle and rooftop owners in our city faced painful trade-offs: handing car keys to colony cleaners who scratch paint with dirty cloths and hard tap water, or watching rooftop solar panels lose 25% of their electricity output due to thick dust and scaling.

We built CWP Detailers to establish a scientific standard right at your gate: Treated soft-water wash system, 2-bucket scratch-free microfiber cleaning for cars, and specialized non-abrasive soft wash for solar panels.

Today, over 1,200 Varanasi families wake up to a gleaming car and peak solar generation without lifting a finger. If you ever have any feedback or question, I am just one WhatsApp message away."
