Google Apps Script Deployment

Deploy CWP Staff Information Viewer

Follow these 5 simple steps to connect the app with your Google Sheet in less than 2 minutes.

1
Open Google Apps Script in your Google Sheet

1. Open your Google Sheet: CWP Google Sheet Link

2. In the top menu bar, click Extensions → Apps Script.

3. A new Apps Script editor tab will open.

2
Paste Code.gs

1. In the Apps Script editor, open the existing Code.gs file (or replace all code inside it).

2. Copy the full content of Code.gs from your project folder using the button below:

Code.gs
Loading Code.gs snippet...
3
Add Index.html

1. In Apps Script, click the + (Add file) button next to Files, and choose HTML.

2. Name the file Index (it will become Index.html).

3. Copy and paste the full content of Index.html using the button below:

Index.html
Loading Index.html snippet...
4
Deploy as a Web App

1. Click the blue Deploy button at the top right → New deployment.

2. In the modal, click the gear icon (Select type) and choose Web app.

3. Set the configuration:

  • Description: CWP Staff Information Viewer
  • Execute as: Me (your email)
  • Who has access: Anyone (this allows staff to view their cards directly from their phones without requiring Google accounts)

4. Click Deploy.

⚠️ When deploying for the first time, Google will ask you to "Authorize Access". Click Review permissions → choose your Google account → click Advanced → click Go to CWP Staff Information Viewer (unsafe) → click Allow.
5
Start Using Your App

Google Apps Script will provide your Web App URL (looks like https://script.google.com/macros/s/.../exec).

  • Admin Page: Open Web App URL or Web App URL?admin=1 to add staff, upload photos, and add customers.
  • Staff Unique Links: Share Web App URL?staff=STAFF_ID directly via WhatsApp from the Admin page!
